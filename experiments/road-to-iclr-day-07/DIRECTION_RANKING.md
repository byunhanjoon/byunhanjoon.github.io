## Highest insight

Side information is not intrinsically useful. Its value is conditional on the
residual task left by the ordinary columns, the learned base, and the semantic
state shift. A correct hierarchy or metric can therefore hurt. This explains
the Day-4/6 negative transfers and the Day-7 architecture-specific harms.

The same standard must be applied to novelty: a successful neural mechanism is
not a paper contribution if a neighboring literature already owns the
mechanism. The late ACE/neural-process audit changes the portfolio decision.

## 1. Evidence-weighted lead — OrbitCover

Working title: **When Equivalent Tables Train Differently:
Interaction-Balanced Estimation of a Learning-Pipeline Quotient**.

### Claim

Exact schema representations and ordinary stochastic training choices form a
declared nuisance product for the complete learning pipeline. The aligned
prediction barycenter is a quotient estimand. Product fANOVA identifies which
nuisance interactions survive, and strength-matched finite designs estimate
the quotient more efficiently than independent randomization at equal fit
budget. Independent cover cross-scores estimate quadratic quotient risk
without the ordinary finite-ensemble upward bias.

### Why it leads

- The theory contains exact Hilbert-risk, fANOVA-filter, finite-population,
  selection-regret, cross-score, and packing-covariance identities.
- The evidence package contains 140,592 audited fit keys over classification,
  regression, multiclass, MLP, ResNet, FT-Transformer, TabM, TabPFN, and tree
  families.
- The final common-panel closure includes 144 neural cells and 12 sources;
  the complete audit passes 116/116 checks.
- The strongest defensible result is not generic schema invariance. Coupled
  OC2 reduces quotient residual 55.9% versus canonical independent draws on
  144/144 cells and 12/12 sources, while independent OC2 loses by 7.0%.
  Coupling and the changed estimand are therefore part of the phenomenon.
- Independent cross-scores and disjoint packing connect design covariance to
  score RMSE and model-selection fidelity with unusually complete mechanism
  checks.

### Limits that must headline the paper

- At convergence, mean OC2/SRS residual ratio is `1.002`; relative efficiency
  does not persist universally.
- Exact function matching closes MLP/ResNet schema effects and nearly closes
  TabM; the persistent component is architecture-conditional.
- Ordinary predictive gains are small and exact validation/test winners agree
  in only 19/36 partitions; this is not an accuracy-SOTA paper.
- Orthogonal arrays, group averaging, U-statistics, and antithetic variance
  reduction are classical. Recent antithetic-CV work prevents broad novelty or
  optimality claims. The residue is the finite complete-pipeline nuisance
  estimand and its exact-to-downstream composition.

### Decision

Write this paper now. Use the Day-5 authoritative closure rather than rerunning
or cherry-picking older positive panels. The paper should say **partially
supported** and defend a precise quotient-estimation contribution.

## 2. Theory/safety companion — conditional value of optional field biases

Working title: **Should This Table Use Its Metadata?**

The exact residual-value identity and same-metadata/opposite-target
impossibility are clean and important. The four-backbone test, however, fails
operator calibration (`rho=0.514`, sign accuracy `61.8%`) and includes an
FT-Transformer harm of `-0.01203`. A clean disjoint-state worst-backbone rule
has zero harms and `+0.00400` mean gain but selects only ACS, failing its frozen
two-source actionability gate.

Decision: retain the theory as a motivating example of why quotient/model
uncertainty matters and the corrected LCB as a safety baseline. Do not submit
it as the standalone method without genuinely fresh sources and a justified
state-shift model.

## 3. Learned component — latent optional-structure PFN

The frozen neural screen succeeds technically:

- 9/9 wins over a geometry-free set transformer;
- `+0.17639` mean MSE advantage across all three seeds;
- `0.00759` mean regret to the analytic Bayes mixture;
- implicit trust/posterior correlation `0.950` and regime AUROC `0.863`.

The theoretical identity is also useful: soft-router regret equals squared
posterior error times squared expert disagreement.

But ACE (AISTATS 2025) explicitly represents latent variables and demonstrates
amortized GP kernel selection; transformer neural processes and FlowNP train
over kernel families; older work amortizes GP hyperparameter/kernel inference.
Thus “a transformer learns which kernel generated the task” is occupied. The
remaining tabular residue is narrower: infer the *incremental residual value*
of externally supplied field structure after ordinary columns and under
complete unseen states. The cycle/Fourier experiment does not establish that.

Decision: keep the implementation, theorem, prior-shift stress test, and
figures as a mechanism appendix or future component. Do not promote it without
a real residual-task bridge and direct ACE/TNP/GP baselines.

## Other directions

- **Measure-valued fields:** principled atomic/continuous decomposition, but
  narrow activation and strong collision with random effects/GAMs.
- **Metric Chart Transport:** useful realization of a cold-state residual
  operator, but largely kernel ridge in an embedding chart.
- **Semantic arithmetic:** causal numerical effect with no mean accuracy gain
  and narrow hardware/software scope.

## Final portfolio decision

Depth effort now goes to an honest, compact OrbitCover submission package.
The conditional-value theory becomes the motivating failure mode; the learned
PFN becomes a clearly labeled exploratory extension. If an expert novelty
review rejects the finite-pipeline quotient composition, no current Day-7
direction has enough evidence and novelty simultaneously, and the program
should generate a new idea rather than disguise a collision.
