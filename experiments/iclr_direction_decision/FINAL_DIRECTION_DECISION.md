# Final ICLR direction decision

## Decision in one paragraph

Commit the next month to Day 3. Exact schema risk is material on all 16 audited
datasets in at least one non-invariant pipeline, while the invariant one-hot
linear control is null. More importantly, the adaptive strength-2 OrbitCover
replicated: it beat all three equal-budget controls in 12/12 initial and 27/27
conditional-confirmation material cells, across all six confirmation source
groups. HeteroBag has a real and useful performance signal—56/72 wins and a
+2.41% pooled mean—but its coordinate-only transformed-T placebo is stronger
on the frozen mechanism triplet, so the claimed semantic representation axis
is not isolated. FieldRiesz retains the highest mathematical upside but fails
its independent semantic hierarchy.

## Evidence that controls the decision

### Day 3 / OrbitANOVA and OrbitCover

- The exact-schema atlas covers 16 datasets, five pipeline families, four seeds,
  and feature/category/class nuisance products. All 16 datasets have at least
  one material test cell; the one-hot linear negative control has zero material
  cells. Adam MLP is material on 16/16 datasets, ordinal forest on 13/16,
  CatBoost on 7/16, and HistGradientBoosting on 3/16.
- The first Cartesian leave-one-dataset-out audit→action policy failed: only
  2/18 material action cases beat iid schema sampling and ordinary seed
  averaging without a loss violation. This result is not rescued or renamed.
- The adaptively frozen budget-4 orthogonal cover passed on the initial panel
  and then on four of six new source groups; at cell level it beat both iid and
  seed-only in 20/27 confirmation material cells.
- The adaptively frozen budget-16 strength-2 cover is the decisive result. It
  beat iid-16, four independent strength-1 covers, and four schema draws each
  averaging four seeds in 27/27 confirmation material cells and 6/6 source
  groups. Mean residual reductions were 70.15%, 59.25%, and 59.63%,
  respectively. The exact source-group sign probability is 0.03125.
- HPO selection is schema-dependent: ordinary per-schema selection has 0.731
  bits mean choice entropy and switches away from the identity choice on 29.3%
  of representatives. But quotient-HPO failed its accuracy gate: it cut mean
  schema risk by 28.1% in 5/8 cells while worsening panel Brier by 0.436%, above
  the frozen 0.1% rope.
- Canonicalization closed all 576 checked exact views to one digest but was
  usually no more accurate than the identity seed ensemble. TabPFN's default
  eight-member internal ensemble reduced schema risk on all four datasets by
  63.5% on average but did not close it and worsened reference Brier by 1.82%
  on average. These are useful controls, not headline wins.

The novelty claim is intentionally narrow. Functional ANOVA is established
mathematics, proper-score variance decompositions are established, randomized
orthogonal arrays are classical variance-reduction devices, and target-label
equivariance has a direct TabPFN treatment. The defensible contribution is the
joined object: an aligned prediction quotient over exact *complete-pipeline*
schema symmetries and seeds, with exact proper-risk attribution and an
equal-fit balanced estimator validated on held-out source groups. See the
[functional-ANOVA foundation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/lengerich20a.html),
[proper-score decomposition](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/gruber23a.html),
[randomized orthogonal-array precedent](https://www3.stat.sinica.edu.tw/statistica/j2n2/j2n27/j2n27.htm),
and [EquiTabPFN](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/5a66c7adffdbde9dd5e78820cbf6935c-Abstract-Conference.html).

### HeteroBag-3

The new conditional second panel used eight datasets, three architectures, and
three independent triplets. HeteroBag beat T+T+T in 56/72 cells (77.8%), had a
+2.41% unweighted pooled mean, positive pooled means on 7/8 datasets, and a
dataset-bootstrap 95% interval of +0.70% to +4.40%. All three triplets and both
task families were positive.

The central mechanism did not survive its frozen control. On triplet 3,
HeteroBag averaged +1.15% relative to T+T+T, while the coordinate-only
`T+T+transformed-T` placebo averaged +1.71%. Cross-representation error
correlation is descriptively more associated with gain than same-representation
error correlation (Spearman -0.683 versus -0.517), but this was not tested on a
third untouched panel and does not override the placebo failure. General
training-method diversity and parameter-efficient tabular ensembling are also
well occupied by prior work, including [representation-diverse ensembles](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BK-4qbGgIE3)
and [TabM](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/c1ba41c694834aeef91ae161711d4939-Abstract-Conference.html).

Protocol limitations are material: test metrics printed incrementally rather
than in one final reveal; alternate rank models missed exact parameter equality
by at most 0.507%; TabM was not run; and the second panel reuses datasets from
earlier frozen multi-view studies. Every frozen cell still completed, and none
was selected, replaced, or stopped based on partial output.

### FieldRiesz

FieldRiesz has the strongest mathematical concept—declared field topology,
mass and stiffness forms, chart covariance, and exact-spectrum controls—but the
performance hierarchy does not replicate. The broad direct panel beats raw
RAPLE in only 17/45 cells and 5/15 dataset–model aggregates. The predeclared
King County replication beats raw RAPLE in 2/9 cells; its mean gaps versus node
and exact-isospectral controls are -0.27% and -0.03%. On Bike, the correct ring
does not consistently separate from the permuted/wrong ring. Keep the
mathematics and covariance tests as a mechanism/appendix or as an admissible
Day-3 action, not as a performance-method paper.

## Required questions

1. **Which direction has the strongest ICLR-level novelty?** FieldRiesz has the
   highest unconditioned mathematical novelty. Among empirically surviving
   directions, Day 3 has the strongest ICLR-level novelty because its exact
   schema/seed quotient estimand and complete-pipeline balanced estimator form
   a differentiated, coherent contribution even after subtracting classical
   fANOVA, proper-risk, and orthogonal-array ingredients.

2. **Which has the strongest prospective empirical evidence?** Day 3. Its
   strength-2 action replicated in every material cell and every one of six new
   source groups. HeteroBag's raw effect is strong but conditionally prospective
   and fails the decisive placebo gate. FieldRiesz fails independent replication.

3. **Which has the clearest reviewer-defensible claim?** Day 3: “A strength-2
   balanced cover over declared exact schema factors and training seed is an
   unbiased estimator of the fitted-predictor quotient and, on six held-out
   source groups, has lower expected Brier/MSE overhead than three equal-fit
   sampling controls.”

4. **Which results replicated?** Day-3 schema risk appeared on all 16 datasets;
   budget-4 cover transfer passed in four of six source groups; budget-16
   strength-2 cover passed in six of six. HeteroBag's T+T+alternate advantage
   replicated across three triplets and both tasks, but its semantic-mechanism
   interpretation did not. FieldRiesz's California hierarchy did not replicate
   on King County or Bike.

5. **Which hypotheses were falsified?** A simple Cartesian audit→action policy
   does not transfer reliably; quotient-HPO is not an accuracy-safe default;
   canonicalization is not generally an accuracy improvement; internal TabPFN
   ensembling does not exactly close schema risk; HeteroBag's gain is not shown
   to require meaningful representation heterogeneity; and FieldRiesz's correct
   semantic geometry does not reliably beat mass, wrong, and isospectral controls.

6. **What is the strongest paper title/one-sentence claim for each surviving
   direction?** Day 3: *OrbitCover: Auditing and Averaging Exact Schema
   Nuisances in Tabular Learning Pipelines* — exact schema spellings and seeds
   form a measurable prediction-risk product, and a pairwise-balanced cover
   estimates its quotient more efficiently than iid or seed-only averaging.
   HeteroBag secondary: *A Representation View Is Often Worth More Than a Third
   Seed* — across a conditional eight-dataset panel, replacing one T-PLE seed by
   Q-PLE or midrank often improves equal-fit bags, but generic coordinate
   perturbation remains an unresolved alternative explanation.

7. **What is the single biggest remaining rejection risk?** The decisive
   OrbitCover action was adaptively introduced after the original Cartesian
   policy failed, and its realistic-schema confirmation spans multiple pipeline
   families but not the full requested MLP/ResNet/FT-Transformer/TabM neural
   matrix. A reviewer can reasonably call the current evidence an excellent
   adaptive discovery rather than a final prospective confirmation.

8. **If only one project can receive the next month of work, which one and why?**
   Day 3. Spend the month on one preregistered, untouched modern-neural panel
   that directly repeats the fixed strength-2 cover, canonical, iid, and
   seed-block controls. That closes the only major evidentiary gap on the
   highest-scoring direction; more HeteroBag tuning would chase a failed
   mechanism gate, and more FieldRiesz performance work would chase failed
   replication.
