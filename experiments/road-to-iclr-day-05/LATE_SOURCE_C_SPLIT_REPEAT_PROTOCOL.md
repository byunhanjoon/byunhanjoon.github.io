# Source-C alternate-partition protocol

Frozen after the original source-C outcomes. This is a conditional partition
repeat, not an independent source or model-family confirmation.

Refit all four source-C datasets and all five established model families over
the same complete `4 x 4 x 2 x 4 = 128` nuisance product using previously
unused stratified split seed `2026083051`. No failed source/model cell may be
replaced, and each manifest must record the effective split seed.

Reuse the original frozen gates on the alternate split: strength-2 must beat
IID-16 and four strength-1 blocks in at least 16/20 cells, beat all three
controls on at least 80% of material cells, and win all 4/4 source means. Each
packing comparison must improve at least 80% of nondegenerate candidates,
have no adverse nondegenerate candidate, and win all 4/4 source means.

Validation/test exact winner agreement and regret have no gate. The combined
two-split agreement is descriptive because the original outcome was known
when this repeat was specified.
