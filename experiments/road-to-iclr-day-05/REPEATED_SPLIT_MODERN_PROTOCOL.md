# Repeated-split modern-model protocol

Frozen after the original-split HistGB/CatBoost outcomes. This is a conditional
partition-transport experiment, not a new-source or new-model confirmation.

## Frozen panel

Reuse all eight late OpenML source identities, the two added model families,
the nuisance menu, and the four model seeds. Refit the complete 128-cell tensor
under three previously unused stratified split seeds:

`2026082901`, `2026082911`, and `2026082921`.

This adds 48 tensors and 6,144 complete-product fits. No failed split, source,
or model may be replaced. Each artifact must record its effective split seed.

## Analyses

For each split separately, rerun strength-2, pair32, pack64, and unbiased
pair-cross64. Then cluster by dataset and split rather than treating actions as
independent observations.

Frozen transport criteria:

- strength-2 beats IID-16, strength-1, and seed blocks on at least 80% of all
  material dataset/model/split cells and on at least 22/24 dataset/split means;
- each packing comparison has no adverse nondegenerate cell and improves at
  least 22/24 dataset/split means;
- report exact validation-to-test winner agreement and regret over all 24
  dataset/split pairs, but impose no pass gate because partition rank transfer
  was already identified as a failure boundary.

The original split remains separate and is used only as a labeled fourth-split
sensitivity roll-up after the three-seed criteria are evaluated.
