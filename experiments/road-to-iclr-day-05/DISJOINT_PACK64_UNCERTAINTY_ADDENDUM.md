# Four-pack source and 128-cell addendum

Status: frozen after the four-pack panel gate and before inspecting this
aggregation.

Compare mutually disjoint four-cover packs with two independent disjoint pairs
at 64 fits using equal-source bootstrap intervals for score RMSE and direct
prediction residual. Separately restrict to the 128-cell candidates, where the
four-pack is not full enumeration.

The addendum passes if both source intervals exclude zero favorably in at least
four of five panels and the 128-cell subset has lower mean RMSE and direct
residual. Exact-closure and non-enumerating evidence must remain separate.
