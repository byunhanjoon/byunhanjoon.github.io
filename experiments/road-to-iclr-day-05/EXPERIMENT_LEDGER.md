# Day-5 experiment ledger

Evidence labels:

- **confirmatory** — gate frozen before the reported outcome;
- **prospective** — new panel/config frozen before fitting;
- **conditional repeat** — changed nuisance/data after an earlier result;
- **post-gate** — scope, mechanism, or stronger-control extension;
- **diagnostic** — explanatory analysis with no confirmatory gate.

“Pass” always refers to that experiment's written gate, not universal success.

## Evidence-weighted bottom line

The primary surviving direction is the nuisance-quotient/cover/cross-score
chain. The exact risk estimator is far more robust than the predictive-SOTA
claim: absolute Brier/MSE gains are small, accuracy is mixed, and held-out
selection can be dominated by validation-to-test rank instability. The
failures above
are part of the scientific result and should remain visible in any paper.
