## Purpose

The empirical cover gains should follow the spectral fANOVA theorem, and the
same theorem must expose counterexamples. This analysis is not another model
benchmark. It locates real pipeline tensors in the exact interaction-energy
phase diagram and verifies the predicted risk algebra.

## Frozen calculations

For the `4 x 4 x 2 x 4` product, compute the independently level-randomized
strength-1/2/3 covariance multipliers. For each pure interaction order, report
the equal-budget residual ratio relative to IID. In particular:

- strength 2 at budget 16 is compared with IID-16;
- strength 3 at budget 64 is compared with IID-64.

Construct scalar, pure-fANOVA tensors from centered factor contrasts and
verify by exhaustive design-family averaging that empirical squared estimator
error equals the spectral multiplier times component energy.

Then combine the already saved strength-2 and strength-3 decomposition tables
for the validation-screened confirmation and untouched OpenML panels. Recover
order-1/2, order-3, and order-4 energy fractions and compare analytical risk
ratios with the independently computed Gram-matrix residual ratios.

The gate requires maximum algebra/Gram discrepancy below `1e-10`, correct
annihilation through the declared strength, and explicit adverse corners:
strength 2 must lose to IID-16 on a pure triple tensor, and strength 3 must
lose to IID-64 on a pure four-way tensor. A method that only demonstrates its
favorable region fails this protocol.
