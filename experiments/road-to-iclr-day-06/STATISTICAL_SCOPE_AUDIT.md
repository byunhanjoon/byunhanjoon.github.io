## Inference policy

- Exact-zero and algebraic-closure results are implementation statements, not
  estimates of a stochastic population rate.
- Dataset-balanced means/ranks prevent a large table or a repeated-view count
  from silently dominating pooled summaries.
- Bundle-level AUROC, sensitivity, specificity, and survival fractions are
  useful screen diagnostics.  With only three datasets, they do not justify
  narrow generalization confidence intervals or universal claims.
- Development exclusions for H6, H7, H8, and H9 are immutable and must be listed
  beside every prospective verdict.  Successor evidence cannot be folded back
  into its development examples.
- Sequential hypothesis invention creates selection pressure.  The final
  ranking credits only each direction's own frozen prospective gate and keeps
  failed predecessors visible; it does not treat the best of many tried rules
  as an unbiased discovery estimate.
- The ranking rubric's three-dataset cap remains binding even if all repeated
  paths agree.

## What the final report must not say

- that 93 H7 views constitute `n=93` independent scientific replications;
- that a high pooled H6/H8 AUROC proves transport to unseen datasets;
- that checkpoint-prediction equality proves every unrecorded GPU state was
  bitwise equal;
- that a frozen threshold is a calibrated deployment threshold;
- that a post-failure successor validates the predecessor it replaced.

The appropriate endpoint is an auditable mechanism and a ranked research
direction, followed by external-dataset, hardware, architecture, and truly
independent confirmation—not a completed generalization claim.
