## Lead direction — OrbitCover

- Strongest defensible claim: interaction-balanced coupling over finite semantic
  and learner-nuisance products estimates a distinct semantic quotient
  predictor more efficiently at finite budget.
- Closest collision: orthogonal-array integration, fANOVA/group averaging,
  antithetic sampling, and antithetic Gaussian cross-validation.
- Decisive result: coupled OC2 wins 144/144 neural cells and 12/12 source units
  at budget 16, with 55.9% equal-source residual reduction and clustered 95%
  interval `[38.7%, 73.8%]` across the audited Day-5 closure.
- Failure boundary: independent OC2 is 7.0% worse; the convergence ratio is
  1.002; the estimator partly shifts its target; held-out selection-regret gain
  is small.
- Next experiment: freeze independent rather than coupled gain and held-out
  selection utility as co-primary endpoints on new source units, with target-
  shift accounting before scaling the method.

## Alternative — Semantic Arithmetic / IEA64

- Strongest defensible claim: for exact tabular schema conjugacies on the
  tested stack, schema-interface rounding is a causal injection whose higher-
  precision accumulation prolongs material survival and reduces final orbit
  damage even after some exact paths breach.
- Closest collision: known last-bit neural-training sensitivity, supervised
  permutation metamorphic tests, training-dynamics conjugacy, and wider or
  selective mixed-precision accumulation.
- Decisive result: H7 passes with 95.2% later hits, 96.8% exact early survival,
  and 20.4% final material failures; H9 passes with 51/51 final wins, 36/51
  rescues, zero twofold worsenings, and +0.760% canonical loss.
- Failure boundary: H3 has 0/9 indefinitely exact cells and only 3/6 stable
  controls; Credit's H7 material-failure fraction is 33.3%; no mean accuracy
  benefit is established, hardware scope is narrow, and canonical gathering
  is preferable when metadata exist.
- Next experiment: a composition-randomized panel of at least 12 datasets,
  five architectures, two accelerator generations plus CPU, and FP32/IEA64/
  canonical-gather/compensated arms with randomized timing order and modern
  schedules.

## Novelty and claim boundary

Numerical training sensitivity is established, as is tabular permutation
invariance, supervised metamorphic testing by attribute/class permutation,
learning-dynamics equivalence, and wider or selective accumulation.  The
surviving Day-6 novelty can only be the narrow composition of an exact
parameter-conjugated tabular schema action, a localized arithmetic intervention,
and its prospectively tested finite-horizon consequence.  The literature audit
is subtraction, not a novelty proof.

Supported claims are restricted to the recorded software/hardware stack,
stored checkpoints, and declared data/model panel.  Canonical gathering gives
the stronger exact intervention when action metadata are available.  IEA64
does not improve mean predictive accuracy, and its timing observation is not a
randomized benchmark.

Primary closest-work anchors are Sun et al.'s [JMLR numerical-sensitivity
study](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-0137.html), Xie et al.'s [supervised
metamorphic classifier tests](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3082144/),
Redman et al.'s [equivalent training dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09160),
Sakr et al.'s [accumulator-width analysis](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=BklMjsRqY7),
and El Arar et al.'s [selective mixed-precision
accumulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15568).  The full subtraction appears
in `RECENT_LITERATURE_AUDIT.md`.
